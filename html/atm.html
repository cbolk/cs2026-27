ATM

given a withdrawal amount that is a multiple of €5, determine the minimum number of banknotes required using €50, €20, €10 and €5 notes, and display the quantity of each denomination

solving algorithm

Use as many €50 bills as possible, then cover the remaining amount with as many €20 bills as possible, cover the remaining amount with as many €10 bills as possible, and finally use €5 bills.

Output
n50—
n20—
n10—
n5—
Examples

Digits only, a positive multiple of 5. Any change restarts from the first step.
